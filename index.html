04 / TEACHING

Teaching is
part of who I am.

My academic background is rooted in mathematics and statistics, but teaching has always been about more than the material itself. I enjoy helping students take something that initially feels intimidating and turn it into something they understand and can use.

I believe the best quantitative education connects theory to real-world problems while giving students the confidence to ask questions, make mistakes, and keep working through difficult ideas.

WHY I TEACH

Mathematics and statistics become much more powerful when students understand why something works, not just how to calculate it. My goal is to make quantitative ideas approachable, practical, and engaging while helping students develop the confidence to solve unfamiliar problems.
